About Me

A curious CS student who believes that the best code is written at midnight (with some good crime documentaries playing in the background) 🌙

Hey there! I'm Rahma Jemal, born and raised in the beautiful town of Shashemene 🌿. As the baby of 5 siblings, I'm a 3rd-year Computer Science student at Meda Welabu University, still figuring out this whole coding thing, but I'm having a blast doing it! 🚀

When I'm not debugging my code (which happens more often than I'd like to admit 😅), you'll find me taking peaceful walks, getting lost in true crime documentaries, or discovering new music and podcasts. Being the youngest in my family means I'm naturally curious about everything!

Our favorite family bonding time? When we all find some show to obsess over together or discover something fascinating that gets us all talking for hours. There's nothing quite like that moment when everyone's equally invested in the same thing! 📺✨

And yes, my mom has this special talent for interrupting my coding sessions at the most crucial moments - like right when I'm about to fix a bug! � She'll walk win asking if I've eaten or reminding me about some random thing, and I'm like "Mom, I'm in the zone!" But honestly, I wouldn't have it any other way. Her timing is legendary! 💕

Fun fact: I'm probably the only CS student who runs on pure determination instead of caffeine - coffee and I just don't get along! 🚫☕ But hey, who needs caffeine when you have the thrill of finally understanding why your loop was infinite, and siblings who cheer you on?
